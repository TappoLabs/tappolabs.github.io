Tappo

Auto Tapper · assistive tap automation

Places friendly crosshair targets over any app and injects taps, swipes, and pinches for you — precise, adjustable, and always one button away from stopping. Built on Android's Accessibility API for repetitive and hands-free tapping.

Free to start Record & replay Per-app profiles

adrift

Cognitive shuffle · falling asleep

A faint letter on a dark screen, and calm unrelated words fading in and out one at a time. It's the cognitive shuffle: enough gentle imagining to crowd out worry, without enough to keep you awake. The screen dims itself to black and the app steps aside.

7 free nights No ads, no account No streaks or notifications
Get it on Google Play ▸ Privacy policy

Watercolor Sort

Paint puzzle · pour, sort, solve

Pour colour between tubes until every tube holds a single shade. Easy to pick up, steadily harder to finish. Progress is saved on your device, and a one-time purchase removes the ads if you'd rather play without them.

Free to play Offline progress
